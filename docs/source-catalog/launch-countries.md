## Status definitions

- **Research pending:** official publishers and source documentation have not
  yet been inventoried for that country/category.
- **Permission review pending:** a candidate source is identified but legal,
  licensing, attribution, and access conditions are not yet approved.
- **Verified:** official publisher evidence and permitted access conditions
  have been reviewed.
- **Operational:** an approved connector has been implemented and tested.

Do not treat a country as covered until evidence and tested operational sources
exist for each category. Candidate portals, historic datasets, regional
sources, and grant-call pages remain research leads only; all 30 countries
still have zero verified live OppSift coverage.

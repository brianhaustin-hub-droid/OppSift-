## Coverage definition

- Government coverage requires a validated official procurement or tender source.
- Grants coverage requires a verified funding or programme source with reviewed terms.
- Commercial coverage requires a permission-checked private-sector or supplier opportunity source.
- A live country status is impossible until the country registry, source records, and tested connectors are complete.

## Placeholder policy

Forty-seven distinct products are recorded in the machine-readable catalogue,
including regional APIs, portal leads, award datasets, data catalogues, and
funding/notice listings. Do not treat these as 47 opportunity feeds or
permission-cleared sources. TED, Contracts Finder, World Bank, NSF, TenderNed,
data.overheid.nl, and OCP Registry paths have bounded research retrieval
evidence; no source is permission-approved, customer-connected, or
production-persisted. Most grants, commercial, and country/category rights
research remains pending.
